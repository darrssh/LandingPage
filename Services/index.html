END-TO-END COLLECTION SOLUTIONS

Our Services

Flexible, stage-based collection support designed to meet the needs of banks, NBFCs, fintechs and lending institutions.

WHAT WE OFFER

Collection Services

Each service can be engaged independently or combined into a comprehensive recovery program.

01

Tele-Collection

Professional calling support for payment reminders, follow-ups and resolution conversations. Our trained agents handle inbound and outbound calls using approved scripts that balance firmness with respect.

  • Outbound reminder and follow-up calling
  • Inbound customer support lines
  • Call recording and quality monitoring
  • Multi-language agent coverage
02

Field Collection

Coordinated doorstep visits and in-person follow-up where appropriate and authorized. Our field teams operate within strict conduct guidelines and documented visit protocols.

  • Geographically distributed field teams
  • Standardized visit documentation
  • Geo-tagged visit verification
  • Escalation protocols for sensitive cases
03

Legal Coordination

Organized documentation and coordination with qualified legal partners when recovery requires legal intervention. We prepare case files and manage the process so your team stays focused.

  • Case file preparation and documentation
  • Coordination with empanelled legal partners
  • Notice dispatch support and tracking
  • Hearing and status updates
04

Skip Tracing Support

Responsible contact-detail verification using lawful, approved methods to reconnect with customers who have moved or become unreachable.

  • Contact detail verification
  • Address and phone number updates
  • Lawful data-source usage
  • Documented verification trail
05

Early Delinquency

Timely reminders and follow-up strategies designed to encourage early resolution before accounts deteriorate further. Early intervention reduces cost-to-collect and preserves relationships.

  • Day-1 to day-90 bucket management
  • SMS, email and call reminders
  • Soft-collection communication flows
  • Preventive engagement strategies
06

Portfolio Recovery

Structured recovery support for eligible secured and unsecured portfolios. We design recovery programs around account stage, value bands and risk profiles.

  • Written-off portfolio recovery
  • Secured and unsecured exposure
  • Value-band based prioritization
  • Performance-based engagement models
07

Receivables Management

End-to-end support for commercial and retail receivables, helping your business maintain healthy cash flow without straining customer relationships.

  • B2B receivables follow-up
  • Ageing analysis and prioritization
  • Payment plan coordination
  • Reconciliation reporting
08

Customer Connect Programs

Relationship-preserving outreach that re-establishes contact, confirms intent to pay and negotiates mutually workable resolution plans.

  • Resolution plan negotiation
  • Restructuring coordination
  • Settlement discussions
  • Customer retention focus
09

Analytics, MIS & Reporting

Clear, configuration-driven reporting and activity analytics that give your team visibility into portfolio performance and support informed decisions.

  • Account and contactability analytics
  • Promise-to-pay and resolution tracking
  • Performance outcome dashboards
  • Client-configurable reporting cadence
