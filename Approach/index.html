OUR METHODOLOGY

How We Work

A practical process designed around accountability and consistent follow-through at every stage.

01

Account Onboarding

We begin by agreeing on scope, permissions, data-handling expectations and priorities. Account allocations, bucket definitions and reporting formats are finalized before any customer contact begins.

02

Customer Outreach

Approved communication channels are used with professional, respectful language. Every attempt is logged, and customer preferences regarding contact times and modes are respected.

03

Follow-ups & Engagement

Outcomes are recorded, and appropriate next steps are coordinated. Promise-to-pay commitments are tracked, and unresolved accounts receive structured, escalating follow-up.

04

Payment Updates

Reported outcomes are reconciled with the client. Payment confirmations, discrepancies and settlement details are documented and shared in agreed reporting cycles.

05

Reporting & Review

Activity, exceptions and improvement opportunities are reviewed together. Performance against agreed KPIs is discussed, and strategies are refined for the next cycle.
