COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked

Quick answers to what partners usually want to know before engaging.

How quickly can you start?

Once scope and permissions are agreed, onboarding of an initial portfolio can typically begin within one to two weeks, depending on volume and integration needs.

Do you work on a pay-for-performance basis?

Yes. We offer pay-for-performance, managed operations and hybrid commercial models so you can choose the structure that fits your risk appetite.

How is customer data protected?

Access is role-based, data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and activity is logged for audit. Data is used only for authorized collection purposes.

What reporting will we receive?

Reporting is configured around agreed KPIs and can include activity, contactability, promise-to-pay, resolution and reconciliation summaries at your preferred frequency.

Can we start with a small pilot?

Yes. We can begin with a defined subset of your portfolio to validate approach, scripts and reporting before scaling to larger volumes.

What data do you need to begin?

A standard file with account details, outstanding balances, contact information and any account history. Confidentiality terms are in place before any data is shared.
